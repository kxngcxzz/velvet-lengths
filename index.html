FAQs

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched within 2–3 working days and usually arrive in 7–14 days in the UK. Delivery is free. You get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

Can I dye or bleach the hair?

Yes, it is real human hair, so it can be toned, curled and heat styled. Darker shades can be lifted by a professional colourist. Always do a strand test first.

How much hair do I need?

For a full head most people need 150–250g: 5 or more packs of nano rings (50g), 2–3 packs of tape-ins (20 pieces each), or 150–200 U-tip strands. For added volume only, half that is usually enough.

What is your returns policy?

Return unopened, unused hair within 14 days of delivery. See our returns policy for how to send it back.
