Questions

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched within 1–3 working days and arrive in 3–7 working days in the UK. You get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

Can I dye or bleach the hair?

Yes. Our hair is unprocessed natural colour 1B, so it can be lifted, toned and heat styled. We recommend a strand test and a professional colourist for anything past level 8.

How many bundles do I need?

Three bundles suit most full heads up to 22". For 24" and longer, or for a very full look, choose four. Add a closure or frontal for a leave-out free install.

What is your returns policy?

Return unopened, unworn hair with the lace uncut within 14 days of delivery. Start a return from your account page.
