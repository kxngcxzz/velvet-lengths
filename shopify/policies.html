Refund policy

Settings → Policies → Refund policy

Changed your mind? You can return unopened hair within 14 days of delivery for a full refund.

What you can return

  • Hair in its original, sealed packaging.
  • Hair that hasn't been opened, worn, washed, cut, coloured or heat styled.

What we can't take back

For hygiene reasons, we can't accept hair once the seal has been broken, unless it's faulty. Please check your colour and length are right before opening the pack.

How to return

  1. Email hello@velvetlengths.uk within 14 days of delivery with your order number and what you'd like to return.
  2. We'll reply with the return address and instructions.
  3. Send the hair back within 14 days of contacting us, using a tracked service. Keep your proof of postage.
  4. We refund you within 14 days of receiving the return, or of receiving proof that you've sent it, to your original payment method.

Return postage is paid by you unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong thing.

Faulty or wrong items

If your hair arrives damaged, sheds heavily, or isn't what you ordered, email hello@velvetlengths.uk within 30 days with photos. We'll arrange a replacement or a full refund, including postage. Your rights under UK consumer law are not affected.

Shipping policy

Settings → Policies → Shipping policy

Every order ships free with tracking, to UK addresses only.

How it works

  1. You place your order and get a confirmation email.
  2. We prepare and dispatch your hair within 2–3 working days.
  3. We email you a tracking link as soon as it ships.
  4. Your parcel usually arrives within 7–14 days of ordering.

Cost

UK delivery is free on every order. The price you pay at checkout is the total: there's nothing extra to pay on arrival.

Tracking your order

Use the tracking link in your dispatch email to follow your parcel. If you can't find it, check your spam folder, or email hello@velvetlengths.uk with your order number and we'll send it again.

Delays

Busy periods such as Black Friday and Christmas can add a few days. If your order hasn't arrived 21 days after you placed it, get in touch and we'll chase it for you.

Wrong address or changes

Check your delivery address at checkout. If you need to change it or cancel your order, email us as soon as possible. We can make changes until your order has been dispatched.

Privacy policy

Settings → Policies → Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you shop with us, why we need it, and your rights under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Who we are

Velvet Lengths is run by Chantel Mungofa, 19 Juniper Close, Nottingham NG11 8TD, who is responsible for your data (the "data controller"). You can contact us about your data at hello@velvetlengths.uk.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, delivery address, email, phone number and what you bought.
  • Payment details: handled securely by Shopify, which runs our checkout. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email or through our website.

Why we use it

  • To process, deliver and track your order, and handle returns. (Legal basis: to perform our contract with you.)
  • To answer your questions. (Legitimate interests.)
  • To send marketing emails, only if you've opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time. (Consent.)
  • To keep records for tax and accounting. (Legal obligation.)

Who we share it with

We only share what's needed with: Shopify, which runs our checkout and takes payment; our suppliers and delivery companies (your name and delivery address, so they can send your order); and our email provider. We never sell your data. Shopify also processes checkout information under its own privacy policy: https://www.shopify.com/legal/privacy/app-users

Some of our suppliers and service providers are based outside the UK. Where your data is sent abroad, we rely on the safeguards UK law requires.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for 6 years for tax purposes. Messages are kept for up to 2 years.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent at any time. Email us and we'll respond within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
